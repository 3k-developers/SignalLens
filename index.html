Privacy Policy for SignalLens

Effective date: October 18, 2026 · Developer: 3kdevelopers

SignalLens analyzes nearby Wi-Fi networks on your device. Wi-Fi scan results, preferences, and diagnostic results remain on your device unless you choose to export or share them. The app uses Firebase Analytics for limited app-use and device telemetry.

Information handled on your device

SignalLens may access or create the following information locally to provide its features:

  • Nearby Wi-Fi details, including network names, BSSIDs, signal strength, channel, and security information.
  • Connection details and locally discovered network-device information shown by the diagnostics tools.
  • App preferences, scan history, and diagnostic results.

This information is stored on your device. It is not sent to 3kdevelopers or Firebase as part of Wi-Fi scanning or diagnostics. Device backups may include app data if you enable Android backup for your device.

Firebase Analytics

SignalLens uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand aggregate app usage and improve the app. Firebase may collect and process information such as app interactions, app version, device and operating-system information, an app-instance identifier, approximate location derived from IP address, and IP address. SignalLens does not send Wi-Fi scan results, diagnostic output, or exported files to Firebase Analytics.

Google processes this information under the Google Privacy Policy.

Network diagnostics and exports

Diagnostics run only when you start them. Ping, DNS lookup, and local-device discovery send requests to the target, local network, or DNS resolver you select or are connected to. Those services and networks may process the request information under their own policies.

Exports are created only when you choose to export or save a CSV file. You control the destination and any sharing of that file.

Permissions

  • Location and Nearby devices: used by Android to allow nearby Wi-Fi scanning and local-network diagnostics.
  • Wi-Fi and network access: used to read Wi-Fi connection information and perform features you request.
  • Internet access: used for Firebase Analytics and diagnostics you initiate.

You can manage permissions in your Android device settings. Some features will not work without the relevant permission.

Data sharing and retention

3kdevelopers does not sell Wi-Fi scan data or diagnostic results, does not provide accounts, and does not use advertising SDKs. Analytics data is processed by Google Firebase as described above. Local app data remains until you clear it, uninstall the app, or remove it through Android settings; Google retains Analytics data according to its applicable policies.

Children

SignalLens is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact 3kdevelopers through the 3kdevelopers Google Play developer page.
